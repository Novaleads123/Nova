# Brand and name due diligence: Nova Leads

> **Status:** Preliminary web research dated 18 November 2026.
> This document is not legal advice or formal trademark clearance. Consult a
> qualified intellectual-property attorney and the appropriate national or
> regional trademark registries before using a name commercially.

## Working conclusion

**Nova Leads** appears less crowded than **Nova CRM** as a search term, but it
is still a generic combination and exact-domain ownership is a separate concern.
The name can be used for early product and repository work, subject to an
official trademark search and rapid, consistent visual-brand registration.

**Nova CRM** should be avoided for this product: search results show multiple
active CRM vendors using that name or close variants across real estate and
general CRM markets.

## Nova Leads search findings

The web search returned no clear evidence of a major, established CRM or
lead-generation product using the exact name “Nova Leads” in global or African
markets. No high-profile App Store or Google Play listing was identified under
that exact name.

Search signals also showed fragmented use in small agencies, campaigns, and
business listings. Some NovaLeads domain permutations appear registered or
minimally developed, but this search did not verify ownership, traffic, renewal,
forwarding, or active commercial use.

Potentially relevant low-risk findings:

- No prominent exact-name SaaS or CRM product was identified.
- No exact-name app-store listing was identified.
- No official trademark registration could be confirmed in the searched sources.
- Search engine absence does not prove freedom to operate.
- Domain availability must be tested independently with a registrar.

## Nova CRM conflicts

Several products use Nova CRM or NovaCRM as active brands:

- **NovaCRM** is a real estate CRM offered as software for agents and brokerages.
  - Capterra listing: https://www.capterra.com/p/10020468/Nova-CRM/
- **Nova CRM by Future Solutions Dev** is offered for service businesses and
  has regional MENA presence.
  - https://futuresolutionsdev.com/en/products/nova-crm
- **NovaHub** includes a CRM module branded “Nova CRM.”
  - https://site.novahubapp.net/nova-crm.html

These findings demonstrate substantially greater name congestion for
“Nova CRM” than for “Nova Leads.”

## Recommended public-facing brand

Retain **Nova Leads** for the MVP and repository only while:

1. A qualified advisor performs trademark screening.
2. A registrar checks exact domains and common misspellings.
3. Social and app-store handles are reserved for the official brand.
4. The visual identity is distinctive enough to avoid generic “Nova” confusion.
5. A fallback name is prepared before launch.

A fallback name should combine Nova with a distinctive geographic or product
signal if trademark or domain checks reveal conflict. Examples are exploratory
only and require their own review:

- Nova Prospect
- Nova Sales Intelligence
- Nova Revenue
- LeadNova
- Nova Africa Leads

## Domain and handle checklist

Before announcing the name publicly, verify:

- novaleads.com
- novaleads.ai
- novaleads.co
- novaleads.africa
- novaleads.io
- novaleadscrm.com
- Matching Google Play and Apple App Store product names
- Matching social handles
- USPTO, WIPO, and relevant African national trademark registries
- Trademark records in Kenya, Nigeria, South Africa, Ghana, and Egypt

## Ongoing monitoring

Record and repeat searches every 3–6 months for:

- `"Nova Leads" CRM`
- `"Nova Leads" lead generation`
- `"Nova Leads" app`
- `"Nova Leads" trademark`
- `"NovaCRM"`
- `"Nova CRM" Africa`

Use official national and regional registries for legal conclusions; search
engines and review platforms are useful discovery sources, not clearance tools.
